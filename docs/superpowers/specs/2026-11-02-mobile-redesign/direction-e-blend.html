Direction E · Midnight Sorbet

Final candidate: C's structure, B2's Home. A deep navy band (status bar, title, search, presets) is overlapped by a light sheet with rounded top corners. Dense rows lead with a 40 px solid sorbet status badge plus a pill chip; times and ₹ sit right in tabular figures. No coloured side rails, no card outlines. Brand indigo is the one action colour. Module colours (Tasks lilac, Complaints peach, Leads mint, Follow-ups butter) are accents: Home tiles, module badges, the active-tab pill.

Decisions

  • Quick-create = option (b): C's 5-tab bar stays; "+ New" sits in the navy band and opens the Create sheet (4b). A raised centre "+" would need a sixth slot or push More into the avatar, and it collides with the bar turning into the record action bar (11). Role-cut bars (26, 27) also stay balanced without a centre hole.
  • Indicator on navy: indigo #3F4FAF on the band measures 2.26 : 1, so the 3 px bar and band underline use Indigo 300 #8592F0 (5.65 : 1). Everything on the light sheet uses #3F4FAF.
  • Active tab: 3 px Indigo 300 bar + icon in its module-colour pill + white label.
